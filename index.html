The salon

Named after an orchid;
run like one.

A Cattleya is a tropical orchid — slow to bloom, fussy about who looks after it, generous in return. The salon takes the same view of a haircut.

35 reviews on Facebook. All five-star.
— quiet, loyal, local

Cattleya is a small independent hair & nails salon on John Street in Luton. Bookings are taken through Facebook Messenger — message the page with what you’re after and we’ll come back with a time.

Hours and prices vary by what you need, so we confirm both on the same message before you come in. Easier than guessing from a price list that wouldn’t fit you anyway.

What we do

Hair, nails, and the time to do them properly.

01 / Hair

For the hair.

Cuts, colour, treatment work — for every length and texture. We talk it through before we lift the scissors. If you’re unsure, bring two reference pictures and we’ll find the version of it that actually suits you.

  • Cut & finish
  • Colour
  • Highlights
  • Blow-dry
  • Treatments
  • Bridal & events
02 / Nails

For the nails.

Manicures, pedicures, gel and acrylic — neat work in a calm chair. A short list of finishes, chosen to actually last the week, on natural nails that don’t end up wrecked.

  • Manicure
  • Pedicure
  • Gel
  • Acrylic
  • Nail art
  • Soak-off

Prices vary by length, condition, and what you’re after — message us on Facebook with a quick description and we’ll come back with a quote and the next free slot.
